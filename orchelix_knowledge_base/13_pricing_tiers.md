# Pricing & Plans — Esmi (Orchelix AI Consulting)

## Pricing Model
Esmi, the AI receptionist, is sold as three monthly plans, each with a
one-time setup fee. Month-to-month, no long-term contract. Paying annually
gets 2 months free and waives the one-time setup fee. Current, authoritative
pricing: https://www.orchelix.com/pricing

---

## Common Questions

**What does the setup fee cover?**
Number, calendar, knowledge base, and go-live onboarding — everything needed
to go live. Waived entirely if you pay annually.

**What happens if I go over my included minutes?**
You're billed the plan's per-minute overage rate. Esmi never stops answering
calls because you've hit a limit.

**Is the monthly service a long-term contract?**
No. Every plan is month-to-month, cancel anytime.

**Can I keep my existing phone number?**
Each plan includes new local number(s). Forwarding or porting your existing
number is usually possible — confirm during onboarding.

**How to get started:**
Start a 7-day pilot ($149) or book a 15-minute walkthrough at
orchelix.com/book.
